# #91 A repair leaks its six audio elements

**state:** open  **labels:** bug, ready-for-agent

## Parent

#81 §7 — cut out of the loop/relay/flow audit.

## What to build

Every repair rebuilds the session's sound player, which constructs six fresh `<audio>` elements. The outgoing player is only muted — never released — so its elements stay alive and decoded.

The reconnect interval's own comment records the measurement: a 6×-throttled client repaired every 2–4.5 s. That is roughly **ninety orphaned audio elements a minute**, on exactly the client that is already struggling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A repair does not leave the previous sound player's audio elements alive
- [ ] Repeated repairs over a long match do not grow the number of live audio elements
- [ ] Sound still works after a repair — the browser suite's existing assertion that every `play()` is recorded in order still holds
- [ ] Music still plays once per room lifetime and is not restarted by a repair

## Out of scope

- Reusing the whole object graph across a repair — measured at under 10 ms all in and rejected, see #70/#73 §3
- The sound settings surface, which is #45
