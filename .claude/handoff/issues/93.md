## What to build

A client's repair allowance and dropped-frame count live on the socket, not on the player's token. A reconnect mints a fresh client record in the admit path, so the counters start at zero again.

A permanently desynced client therefore just reloads the page and its repair budget resets. The cap exists to stop a client that cannot hold sync from costing the room an unbounded number of repairs; today it caps reloads-per-socket instead, which is not a limit at all.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A client's repair allowance survives a reconnect on the same token, rather than resetting with the socket
- [ ] A client that exhausts its allowance and reloads does not get a fresh allowance
- [ ] The allowance still resets at a match boundary, where a fresh match is a legitimately fresh start
- [ ] A client is ejected from the *match* and keeps its seat for the next one, rather than being ejected from the room
- [ ] `test/relay.test.mjs` covers exhausting the allowance, reconnecting and being refused
